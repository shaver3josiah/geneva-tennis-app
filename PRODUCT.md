# Geneva Tennis — product

**What it is.** The team app for Geneva College men's and women's tennis (NCAA Division III,
Presidents' Athletic Conference, Beaver Falls PA). One Expo codebase ships an Android APK and an
iOS TestFlight build. It is a sibling of the Fast Basketball app and keeps all of its team
features: coach ↔ player messaging, the practice calendar, the coach's workout builder, the
Locker, streaks and celebrations, roster invites.

**The flagship.** A match tracker: a phone on the back fence (arm's reach above head height,
~3 m behind the baseline, 0.5x lens, landscape) follows both players and the ball, keeps the
score, measures ball pace, net clearance, topspin, swing speed and depth, reads body shape (legs
and overall form), raises trend alerts with body-shape clips, and keeps the coach's spreadsheet
current every 3 points or at the end of each game.

**Who uses it, and where.**
- The head coach (and assistants) — reads the live sheet and trend alerts on a phone, between
  points and on changeovers, often outdoors in sun; exports to Excel/Google Sheets after.
- Student charters / students — run the tracker, confirm or fix each point with one tap, and
  can email the match file to the coach from their own account.
- Players — read their own matches and form clips after play.

**Truth constraints.** Measurements are estimates from one camera and must say so. Speeds use a
drag + Magnus fit through a court calibration; spin is an estimate. College dual singles are
no-ad, best of three, full third set (ITA rulebook I.B). ITA rule I.M.9 restricts "Player
Analysis Technology" during a match for coaching, so in-match live use needs conference /
ITA clearance; after-match and practice use is unrestricted.

**Brand.** Geneva gold `#C99A2C` (the college's web theme colour), New Gold `#DAAF48`,
charcoal `#232323`, cream `#FBF7EE`. Original mark: a gold tennis ball whose seam is a bold G,
with two wind streaks through its mouth (the Golden Tornadoes' swirl). The college shield and
athletics tornado are Geneva trademarks — not used. Generated by `scripts/brand.mjs`.

**Register.** Operate: scanability, one-tap actions, glanceable numbers at arm's length,
honest labels ("est."), no colour-only signals, 44 pt targets, dark UI for outdoor glare.
